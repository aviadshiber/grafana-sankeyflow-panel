# Code of Conduct

SankeyFlow is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes listening in good faith, giving actionable feedback, crediting others’ work, and prioritizing the health of the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the project’s private contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action needed to keep the community safe.
